   paragraph's description (controlled experiments, behavioral probes).

   pass@1 / 13.4% cons@64; o1 74.4% / 83.3% — replacing the softened "order of 10%" wording,
   per the task's restore-if-confirmed rule. Codeforces 1673/89th pct and GPQA figures were
   already correct.

   658 total and 671B model confirmed).
6. **Ch18 LlamaRL row** (`distributed-sync.md`): 10.4× → **10.7×**, baseline corrected to
   DeepSpeed-Chat-like systems on a 405B policy model; also softened unattested "4096+ GPUs"
   to "thousands of GPUs" (abstract wording).
7. **Ch18 AReaL row** (same file): "2.77× for 671B MoE GRPO" → "up to 2.77× … with matched or
   improved final performance" (abstract wording; 671B MoE unattested).
8. **Ch18 Further Reading** (`data-engineering.md`): four wrong arXiv IDs removed/re-pointed
   (see table). Note the body text (§ "seed counts", OPD route, agent-task expansion) still
