# nt-3 report — notation consistency across Chapters 13–18

## Summary

Audited all prose/math pages in `docs/chapter13_rlhf`, `chapter14_dpo`,
`chapter15_grpo`, `chapter16_reasoning`, `chapter17_prm_search`, and
`chapter18_llm_rl_industrial` for within-chapter symbol consistency against
`docs/appendix_d_math/notation.md`. Fixed 20 files: unified the reference
policy, old/sampling policy, PPO ratio, reward-model score, and KL symbols
within each chapter to a single form (matching the notation page wherever the
chapter majority did not already), defined two previously undefined symbols
($s_i$ in 17.2, and the scope of $\tau$ was already fine), and added six
one-line mapping notes where a chapter legitimately keeps a different
convention (paper-quote notation, $q/o$ vs $x/y$, local reuse of
$\beta$/$\alpha$/$\gamma$/$k$/$n$). No derivations, numbers, or code were
changed. `npx vitepress build docs` succeeds.

Suggest a coordinator-level decision (and a notation-page line) rather than per-worker edits.
