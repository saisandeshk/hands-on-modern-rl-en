Correction: standard-direction ratio with decoupled IS clip bounds applied as a stop-gradient coefficient (`sg(r̂)·Â·log π_θ`), per MiniMax-M1 §3.1 Eqs. 3–5; added the paper's own expansion (Clipped IS-weight Policy Optimization).
