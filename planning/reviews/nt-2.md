# Review of nt-2

## Errors

1. `docs/chapter10_offline_rl/offline-data-distribution-shift.md` — "When the actual gap is below ξ, α is increased to strengthen the conservative penalty. Otherwise α is decreased" — the direction is inverted, and it contradicts the loss printed two lines above. With $g=\mathbb{E}_s[\log\sum_a\exp\hat Q]-\mathbb{E}_{\mathcal D}[\hat Q]$ and $\mathcal{L}(\alpha)=-\alpha(g-\xi)$, the gradient is $\partial\mathcal L/\partial\alpha = \xi-g$, so gradient descent gives $\alpha\leftarrow\alpha+\eta(g-\xi)$: α **increases** when the gap is **above** ξ and decreases when it is below. That is also the CQL dual-ascent direction (the constraint is $g\le\xi$). As written, a gap below target would raise the conservative weight, which pushes the gap further down; the paragraph's claim that "the gap stabilizes automatically near the target" fails. — Suggested fix: "When the actual gap exceeds ξ, α is increased… otherwise α is decreased."

2. `docs/chapter10_offline_rl/experiments.md` — "$\hat{J}_{\text{DR}} = \frac{1}{N}\sum_i[\hat{V}(s_1) + \sum_t \gamma^t \rho_{1:t}(r_{i,t} + \gamma\hat{V}(s_{i,t+1}) - \hat{Q}(s_{i,t}, a_{i,t}))]$" — the edit indexed the inner terms by trajectory $i$ but left the model baseline $\hat V(s_1)$ unindexed inside $\sum_i$, so the formula adds the same single start-state value once per trajectory. It should be $\hat V(s_{i,1})$ (this is also what makes the estimator doubly robust trajectory by trajectory; the two terms must come from the same episode). — Suggested fix: write $\hat V(s_{i,1})$ and, for clarity, $\rho^{(i)}_{1:t}$.

3. `docs/chapter10_offline_rl/experiments.md` (also `docs/chapter10_offline_rl/review.md`, Key Equations) — "$\hat{J}_{\text{PDIS}} = \frac{1}{N}\sum_i \sum_t \gamma^t \rho^{(i)}_{1:t} r_{i,t}$" — the revision now documents that this chapter numbers steps from 1 ($s_1$ is the initial state), but the discount exponent was not shifted: with $t=1$ for the first reward, that reward is discounted once ($\gamma^1$) instead of not at all. Same off-by-one in $J(\pi)=\mathbb{E}[\sum_t\gamma^t r_t]$ (line "the candidate's return …") and in the DR sum in Finding 2. The first term must be $\gamma^0$. For a 100-step episode at $\gamma=0.99$ this scales every estimate by 0.99; for $\gamma=0.9$ by 10%. — Suggested fix: use $\gamma^{t-1}$ in PDIS/DR (and state the sum starts at $t=1$), or re-index the chapter to 0-based rewards.

4. `docs/chapter08_ppo/gae-reward-model.md` — "Schulman et al. proposed GAE (arXiv 2015, ICML 2016)" — the GAE paper ("High-Dimensional Continuous Control Using Generalized Advantage Estimation", arXiv:1506.02438) appeared at **ICLR 2016**, not ICML 2016. — Suggested fix: "arXiv 2015, ICLR 2016".

5. `docs/chapter08_ppo/trust-region-clipping.md` — "$\delta$ is typically 0.01, meaning the policy's behavior distribution may change by at most 1% per update" — KL is measured in nats, not percent, and 0.01 nats is not a 1% bound on probabilities. For a two-action policy, shifting $(0.5,0.5)\to(0.535,0.465)$ already gives $D_{\mathrm{KL}}=0.5\ln(0.535/0.465)+0.465\ln(0.465/0.535)\approx0.0098$, i.e. a 3.5-percentage-point (≈7% relative) change at $\delta=0.01$. The page's own example (0.6→0.99, KL≈1.18) shows how little "1%" tracks the actual probability change. — Suggested fix: "δ=0.01 bounds the *average KL in nats*; it is a small trust-region radius, not a 1% bound on action probabilities."

6. `docs/chapter12_exploration_marl_hierarchical/marl.md` — "`entropy_bonus = -new_logp_list[i].mean()` … `update(actors[i], policy_loss + 0.01 * entropy_bonus)`" — `-mean(log π(a|o))` is a single-sample estimate of the entropy $H$ (positive), so **adding** `0.01 * entropy_bonus` to a loss that is minimized *reduces* entropy. The MAPPO sketch as printed drives the actors toward determinism, the opposite of what the name and PPO's objective intend. — Suggested fix: `update(actors[i], policy_loss - 0.01 * entropy_bonus)` (or compute `entropy = dist.entropy().mean()` and subtract `ent_coef * entropy`, as in §8.4).

## Doubts

1. `docs/chapter09_continuous_control/review.md` — "outputs $\mu = 0$, $\sigma = 1.0$" followed by "(b) the Gaussian log-density $\log \mu(u \mid s)$" — after replacing $\mu_0$ with $\mu$, the same symbol now denotes both the Gaussian mean and the density inside one exercise (the reviewer's own "accepted overload" list covers §9.2/§9.3, but here it is the same sentence). The old $\mu_0$ at least distinguished the mean value. Suggest writing "mean $\mu_\theta(s)=0$" and keeping the density as $\mathcal{N}(u;\mu,\sigma^2)$ or $p(u\mid s)$. Not a numeric error.

2. `docs/chapter12_exploration_marl_hierarchical/review.md` — "PPO does not clip updates that move a bad action's probability down" — true for the worked case $r=1.5$, but false as a general statement: with $A_t<0$ and $r_t<1-\varepsilon$, `min` picks the clipped constant and the gradient is exactly zero (the page's own four-case table says so). The writer flagged this in `planning/reports/nt-2.md` (open issue 4) but left the text unchanged. Suggest "...does not clip *recovery* moves back toward the trust interval".

3. `docs/chapter12_exploration_marl_hierarchical/intrinsic-motivation-exploration.md` — "$(\beta_i, \gamma_i, c_i)$" — $c_i$ is never defined in §12.1 (or anywhere in the chapter), and the new gloss in §12.3 resolves $c$ against the book-wide gradient-clipping meaning rather than against this in-chapter use, contrary to the report's claim that §12.1 "locally defines" it. Either define the Agent57 family parameters or drop the third symbol.

4. `docs/chapter08_ppo/trust-region-clipping.md` — "OpenAI's alignment training in InstructGPT and GPT-4 uses PPO rather than TRPO" — InstructGPT is documented (PPO); the RL algorithm inside GPT-4 was not disclosed in that detail, so "GPT-4 uses PPO" is stated more precisely than the public record supports. Consider limiting the claim to InstructGPT.

## Verdict

The notation pass itself is careful and arithmetically sound: I re-derived the Chapter 7 re-indexing ($G_t=4.9010$, $\delta=1.97$, $5.51/3.9/1$, $5.52/2.8$, $\delta=(4.6,-2.1,1.0)$, $A^{(3)}_0=3.52$, GAE $3.5355$) and the Chapter 7/8/9/10/11/12 exercise solutions, and everything matches; the $\hat A_t$, $p_0$, $D_{\mathrm{KL}}$, $\pi_{\text{old}}$ and gloss changes are internally consistent and the $r_t$ decision matches Chapters 2, 3 and 8 and the notation page. The remaining problems are in surrounding technical claims that the audit should have caught: the CQL α-adjustment direction is inverted relative to both its own loss and the paper, the DR/PDIS formulas are internally inconsistent about indexing (missing $i$ on the baseline; γ^t under 1-based steps), the MAPPO sketch has a sign error that would collapse entropy, one paper venue is wrong (GAE → ICLR 2016), and the "δ=0.01 ≈ 1%" gloss is false. None of these is caused by the notation edits except the partial indexing of the DR estimator, which is the one edit that should be completed.
