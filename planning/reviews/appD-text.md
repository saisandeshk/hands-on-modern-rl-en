## Doubts

1. `docs/appendix_d_math/calculus-derivations.md` — "we hold $Q^\pi(s,a)$ fixed in this simplified one-state derivation (the full policy gradient theorem shows the terms from differentiating the state distribution sum to zero, so only the explicit $\pi_\theta$ dependence survives)" — the qualification is welcome, but the parenthetical is not literally right: in the policy gradient theorem the term $\nabla_\theta d^\pi_\theta(s)$ does not vanish by itself. It is canceled by the corresponding part of the $\theta$-dependence of $Q^\pi$, and the cancellation uses $\sum_a \pi_\theta(a\mid s)\nabla_a Q^\pi(s,a)=0$ (the score-function identity applied to the value function). "The state-distribution terms sum to zero" suggests the terms themselves are zero, which would make the theorem trivially true for any $Q$. — Consider: "the $\theta$-dependence of the state visitation distribution cancels against the $\theta$-dependence of $Q^\pi$ (using $\sum_a \pi_\theta(a|s)\nabla_a Q^\pi(s,a)=0$), leaving only the explicit $\pi_\theta$ dependence."

2. `docs/appendix_d_math/calculus-policy-gradient.md` — the summary row now reads `| Advantage function | $A(s,a)=Q(s,a)-V(s)$ |`, but the body of the same page never introduces $Q$: it computes `$A(s,a)=10-8=2$` from a realized *return* and `$A(s,a)=6-8=-2$`. The formula itself is correct (it matches `A_\pi(s,a)=q_\pi(s,a)-v_\pi(s)` in D.2.5 and `A(s,a) = Q(s,a) - V(s)` in chapter07_actor_critic/actor-critic.md:11), but on this page the same symbol is used for two different objects (a sampled-return estimate and the exact advantage). The report's justification ("consistent with $A=G-V$ used two paragraphs above") does not actually justify the change. — Consider writing the body as $A(s,a)\approx G-V(s)$ (one-sample estimate) and stating in the summary that the definition is $A(s,a)=Q(s,a)-V(s)$.

3. `docs/appendix_d_math/calculus-advanced-formulas.md` (D.3.5) vs `calculus-derivations.md` (D.3.4) and `calculus-formulas-exercises.md` (D.3.6) — the GRPO advantage is written $\hat A_i=(r_i-\mu)/(\sigma+\epsilon)$ in D.3.5 (and in its summary table) but $\hat A_i=(r_i-\mu)/\sigma$ in D.3.4 and D.3.6, inside the same module. Both conventions exist in the literature (the DeepSeekMath paper divides by the group standard deviation; many implementations add a small $\epsilon$ for numerical stability, and $\sigma=0$ for a uniform group is a real degeneracy), so this may be intentional, but a formula reference that gives two different formulas for the same quantity in three consecutive pages is a defect for readers comparing them. The new `notation.md` lists "$\mu$, $\sigma$ (GRPO) | Group mean and standard deviation used to normalize rewards" without noting the $\epsilon$ variant at all. — Consider picking one convention, stating it once in D.3.4, and noting the other in the notation page.

4. `docs/appendix_d_math/linear-algebra-formulas-exercises.md` — "Chapter 2 defined a policy objective $J(\theta)$." — no chapter other than Chapter 6 defines $J(\theta)$ (grep for `J(\theta)` in `docs/chapter02_rl_basics` returns nothing; `docs/chapter06_policy_gradient/reinforce.md:35` is where the formula appears). Chapter 6 is itself inconsistent about this: `reinforce.md:9,33` says "the policy objective $J(\theta)$ **from Chapter 3**" while linking to `../chapter02_rl_basics/policy-value`, and `pg-necessity.md:93` calls the same thing "in Chapter 2". So the appendix's cross-reference may be following one of two contradictory statements in the main text. — Consider pointing at the page that actually defines it (Chapter 6.1) rather than a chapter number, or fixing the main-text references first.

5. `planning/reports/appD-text.md` — item 4 describes the Taylor defect as "malformed. Wrong claim: `f+e...` with missing operators (`f(theta) nabla f^T Delta 1/2 Delta^T H Delta`)" — the original text was not missing operators, it had the wrong signs: `f(\theta) - \nabla f(\theta)^\top\Delta\theta - \frac{1}{2}\Delta\theta^\top H\Delta\theta` (both terms subtracted). The fix (`+` on both lines) is right; only the description of the original error is wrong, which matters because the report is the input for the next reviewer. — Suggested fix: reword to "the two Taylor terms were subtracted instead of added".

6. `docs/appendix_d_math/calculus-derivations.md` — the ratio expansion `r_t(\theta)\approx 1+\nabla_\theta r_t^\top(\theta-\theta_{old})+\frac{1}{2}(\theta-\theta_{old})^\top\nabla^2_\theta r_t(\theta-\theta_{old})` does not say that the derivatives are evaluated at $\theta_{old}$ (where $r_t(\theta_{old})=1$ and the expansion point must be), and the notation $\nabla^2_\theta r_t$ without an evaluation point is ambiguous between $\nabla^2 r_t(\theta_{old})$ and $\nabla^2 r_t(\theta)$. — Consider writing $\left.\nabla_\theta r_t\right|_{\theta=\theta_{old}}$ and $\left.\nabla^2_\theta r_t\right|_{\theta=\theta_{old}}$.

## Verdict

The numeric and derivational core of this revision is solid: I recomputed every changed number and every fix is right (the three discount returns `3.3125/5.9861/6.8909`, the Q-learning target `r+γmax_{a'}Q(s',a')-Q(s,a)`, the advantage `Q(s,a)-V(s)`, the `+`-sign Taylor expansion, the `10·0.9^50 = 0.0515` convergence bound, the "population form" covariance label), and the writer was right to add the semi-gradient caveat to the DQN target and to soften the "Q does not depend on θ" claim. The problems are concentrated in the newly written `notation.md`, which is the part of the change that was not verified: its KL row is broken by an unescaped pipe and will not render (the KL meaning is dropped and a KaTeX error box appears), it claims the book's standard TD-error convention is the one the book actually uses least often (16 vs 5 occurrences), it attributes `ρ_t` to off-policy chapters that never use it and lists a `ρ_{1:t}` symbol that exists nowhere, and the page is not in the sidebar at all, so it ships as an unreachable page with the placeholder number "D.N". Two consistency misses also remain: the writer repaired the D.2 roadmap link texts but left the identical defect in the D.1, D.3 and D.4 roadmaps, and fifteen of the 28 Appendix D pages still have a front-matter `title:` that disagrees with their own H1 (including the D.2.2 page the writer's own D.2 fix touched); the fixed factual errors were also not recorded in `planning/content-review-notes.md` as the repo guidelines require. Errors 1–3 and 5–7 should be fixed before merge; the doubts are wording/convention checks that can be resolved in the same pass.
