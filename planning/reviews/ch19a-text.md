## Errors

1. `docs/chapter19_agentic/credit-assignment.md` — "ground_truth = "40030"  # 2 * pi * 6371 ≈ 40030.14, rounded to the nearest km" — the value does not match the expression it annotates. `2 * pi * 6371 = 40030.1736...` km, so the true value of "2·π·6371" is 40030.17, not 40030.14. 40030.14 is what the *tool* actually computes, because the plan evaluates the literal `2 * 3.14159 * 6371 = 40030.13978` with the truncated constant π ≈ 3.14159. The comment therefore mixes the two constants, and it also contradicts the writer's own report, which states "verified: 2·π·6371 ≈ 40030.17". Minor, but it is a numeric claim added by this revision and it is wrong as written. Suggested fix: `# 2 * 3.14159 * 6371 ≈ 40030.14 (exact 2πR ≈ 40030.17), rounded to the nearest km`.

2. `docs/chapter19_agentic/industrial-evaluation.md` (line 126) and `docs/chapter19_agentic/industrial-practice.md` (line 122) — "$$R = r \times \gamma^{T}$$ … Credit for individual turns then follows the standard discounted-return decomposition $G_t = \sum_{k \geq t} \gamma^{k-t} r_k$" — the two sentences cannot both describe the same trajectory, and the numbers quoted in the sentence match neither convention. If the terminal reward is the one just defined, $r_T = R = r\gamma^T$, then the stated decomposition gives $G_1 = \gamma^{T-1}R = r\gamma^{2T-1}$, i.e. with γ = 0.95, T = 5 → 0.95⁹ = 0.630 (not the 0.77 quoted) and T = 20 → 0.95³⁹ = 0.142 (not 0.36). If instead the terminal reward is the plain binary $r$, the length penalty is already carried by the discounting: $G_1 = \gamma^{T-1}r$ = 0.95⁴ = 0.814 (T = 5) and 0.95¹⁹ = 0.377 (T = 20) — again not 0.77/0.36. The quoted pair (0.77, 0.36) = (γ⁵, γ²⁰) belongs to a third convention (γ^T) that is neither of the two. Net effect: as written, the learner optimizes a return whose length exponent is 2T−1 (a double length penalty) while the text advertises the γ^T convention. Suggested fix: pick one convention and make the numbers follow it — e.g. keep the terminal reward as the binary $r$ and let the discounted return produce the length penalty ($G_1 = \gamma^{T-1}r$, so 0.81 for T = 5 and 0.38 for T = 20 with γ = 0.95), or keep $R = r\gamma^T$ as the only reward and delete the cross-reference to the discounted-return decomposition.

3. `docs/chapter19_agentic/industrial-evaluation.md` (line 763) — "Even so, 0.83 means that nearly 2 out of every 10 judgments still disagree with humans." — Kendall's τ is a rank-correlation coefficient computed over the whole item set; it is not a per-item disagreement rate and cannot be converted into one. τ = 0.83 says that the *ordering* of the LMM judge's scores agrees with the human ordering; it does not imply that 20 % of individual pages are judged differently (that number requires the raw item-level agreement matrix). The page also compares τ against "0.11-0.44" as if a per-item error rate could be read off the same scale. Suggested fix: "a rank correlation of τ = 0.83 still leaves room for substantial item-level disagreement, but that disagreement rate cannot be read off τ directly; it requires the item-level agreement statistics."

4. `docs/chapter19_agentic/multi-agent-swarm.md` (line 103) — "The term $N!$ requires **counterfactual evaluation** — removing agent $i$ from the team and checking whether the task can still be completed." — the attribution is backwards. The Shapley weight $\frac{|S|!(N-|S|-1)!}{N!}$ is a purely combinatorial coefficient and costs nothing to evaluate; what forces counterfactual re-runs is the marginal value $v(S\cup\{i\}) - v(S)$, which must be measured for each of the $2^N-1$ coalitions. The second clause of the sentence describes the marginal-value computation, not the factorial term, so the paragraph contradicts itself. Same block, second problem: Approach 1 defines "the number of agents" as $K$, and the Shapley formula then uses $N$ for the same quantity without saying so — a notation switch inside one section. Suggested fix: "The weight $\frac{|S|!(N-|S|-1)!}{N!}$ is combinatorial; the cost lies in evaluating $v(S\cup\{i\})-v(S)$, which requires re-running the task for each of the $2^N-1$ coalitions. Here $N$ (the number of agents) is the $K$ of Approach 1."

## Doubts

1. `docs/chapter19_agentic/credit-assignment.md` — the section is still titled "Turn-Level Discounting: Earlier Mistakes Carry Greater Responsibility" and opens with "errors in earlier steps have a greater impact … To model this intuition, researchers reuse the standard discounted return", but the standard discounted return does not model that. With γ = 0.9 and a terminal reward at T = 7, $G = [0.531, 0.590, 0.656, 0.729, 0.810, 0.900, 1.000]$; with a terminal −1 the values are the negated list. In both cases the earliest turn carries the *smallest* credit magnitude, i.e. the opposite of "earlier mistakes carry greater responsibility" in terms of credit magnitude. The mechanism that actually makes early errors costly (compounding failure) is not in the equation at all, and the page's own closing note ("identical to the $G_t$ calculation in Chapter 6 for REINFORCE") concedes that the section now adds nothing beyond standard RL. The replacement is technically correct — it is just that the heading and the lead sentence still promise a mechanism the formula does not deliver.

2. `docs/chapter19_agentic/multi-agent-swarm.md` (lines 39, 222, 230) — "Anthropic's internal research published in 2025 measured that the Orchestrator-Worker pattern achieves a 90.2% speedup and an 18–32% increase in success rate on SWE-bench Verified", "Internal data from Anthropic: the 'chain of errors' rate in multi-agent systems is 2.7 times that of single-agent systems", "Anthropic's Debate system enforces that at least one agent must hold a dissenting position". 5. `docs/chapter19_agentic/build-agentic-training-system.md` (line 458) and `code/chapter19_agentic/agentic_system/policy.py` — "As in [DeepSeekMath Equations (3) and (4)], ratio, clipping, and the KL estimator are all computed per token." The code computes `delta = ref_logprobs - new_logprobs = log(π_ref/π_θ)` and `per_token_kl = exp(delta) - delta - 1 = π_ref/π_θ − log(π_ref/π_θ) − 1`, which is the k3 estimator of KL(π_ref ‖ π_θ); DeepSeekMath's GRPO penalty is KL(π_θ ‖ π_ref), whose k3 estimator is π_θ/π_ref − log(π_θ/π_ref) − 1 — the reciprocal ratio. So the estimator does not match the cited equations (only the "per token" part of the sentence does), and with on-policy samples drawn from π_θ the reversed estimator is also the biased direction. Either flip the sign of `delta` or reword the note.

## Verdict

The revision is careful and, on the whole, a clear improvement: the PRM credit table in `credit-assignment.md` really is `[0.427, 0.134, −0.175, −0.500]` for the printed code (`immediate = [0.3, 0.3, 0.3, −0.5]`, γ = 0.95, backward accumulation), the ORM-negative first step really is −0.857, the γ = 0.9 turn returns really are `[0.531 … 1.000]`, the GiGPO mean really is 0.577, and the `code_reward` crash on a `None` observation, the `old_logprobs = new_logprobs.detach()` ratio-is-1 problem, the `s.content contains` syntax error and the wrong A2A/Debate/OpenRLHF attributions are all real and correctly fixed (I re-verified each against `code/chapter19_agentic/agentic_system/*`). The most important remaining problems are the ones the revision itself introduced or left in place: the new Gamma-decay block in 19.6/Supplement is internally inconsistent (R = r·γ^T cannot be combined with the discounted-return decomposition it cites without double-discounting the length penalty, and the quoted 0.77/0.36 follow neither convention), the new `ground_truth` comment states 2π·6371 ≈ 40030.14 when it is 40030.17, the "Turn-Level Discounting" section now presents plain standard RL under a heading that promises the opposite mechanism, and the pre-existing Kendall's τ = 0.83 → "2 out of 10 judgments disagree" inference in the evaluation supplement is a plain misreading of a rank correlation. The 2025–2026 paper ecosystem and the Anthropic/Kimi internal figures remain unverified and, in the multi-agent page, are stated more precisely than anyone could know.
