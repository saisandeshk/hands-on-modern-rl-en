# Review of ch07-text

## Errors

1. `docs/chapter07_actor_critic/ac-frontier.md` — "The full training process lasted several weeks." vs. the table cell "| **Training scale** | hundreds of millions of games, 44 days |" — the revision deliberately softened the AlphaStar scale claims in the "Training Scale and Key Results" paragraph ("several weeks", "a very large number of self-play games", "well over a hundred million parameters"), but the original, more precise numbers survive in two other places of the same page: the league paragraph ("the overall system accumulates hundreds of millions of games") and the final comparison table ("hundreds of millions of games, 44 days"). The page now contradicts itself, and the claims the writer removed as unverifiable (44 days, 12,000 TPU-years) are still printed in the table. Suggested fix: apply the same softening to the league paragraph and to the comparison-table cell (e.g. "millions of self-play games over weeks of league training").

2. `docs/chapter07_actor_critic/review.md` (Exercise 8 solution) — "letting the Actor 'improve' its loss by making the Critic pessimistic" — the direction is wrong. Without `.detach()`, the actor term contributes $\partial L_{\text{actor}}/\partial V = -\log\pi \cdot \partial\delta/\partial V = -\log\pi \cdot (-1) = \log\pi < 0$ (since $\log\pi = -0.6931$), so gradient descent *increases* $V(s)$: the Critic becomes more **optimistic**, which shrinks $\delta$ and drives $-\log\pi\cdot\delta$ down without improving the policy. Verified numerically with the repo's torch (2.14.1): with `.detach()` removed, `V.grad = -4.6531 = -3.96` (critic term) `+ -0.6931` (actor term), i.e. $V$ moves up by $0.1\times0.6931 = 0.069$ per step. The stated numbers in the same solution ($P(a=1): 0.5\to0.5493$, $V: 1.0\to1.396$) are correct — only the diagnosis of the bug is inverted. Suggested fix: replace "pessimistic" with "optimistic (it inflates $V(s)$, shrinking $\delta$ and reducing the actor loss without improving the policy)".

3. `docs/chapter07_actor_critic/pendulum.md` (tip "Tanh squashing and the log-probability correction") — "The code squashes samples with $a = 2\tanh(u)$" contradicts the code printed 20 lines above on the same page: `mu = torch.tanh(self.mu_head(features)) * 2.0` squashes the **mean** into $[-2,2]$; the sample is drawn around it. Worse, the script that produced the reported results (SB3 A2C) does not squash at all: SB3's `MlpPolicy` uses a `DiagGaussianDistribution` with `squash_output=False` and clips the sampled action to the action-space bounds; the tanh Jacobian correction belongs to `SquashedNormalDistribution` (SAC/TD3), not to the A2C policy this section uses. A reader following the tip would add a log-Jacobian term to a loss that never needed it. Suggested fix: state that this section's head squashes the mean and the sampled action is clipped to $[-2,2]$, that SB3's A2C samples an unsquashed Gaussian and clips (so its `log_prob` needs no correction), and that the correction is required only if the *sample* is squashed (as in SAC).

4. `docs/chapter07_actor_critic/pendulum.md` (same tip) — "$\log \pi(a|s) = \log \mathcal{N}(u; \mu, \sigma) - \sum_i \log(1 - \tanh^2(u_i) + \epsilon)$" — for the stated transform $a = 2\tanh(u)$ the Jacobian is $\partial a/\partial u = 2(1-\tanh^2 u)$, so the correct term is $\sum_i \log\!\big(2(1-\tanh^2 u_i) + \epsilon\big)$; the display drops the per-dimension $\log 2$ (the prose mentions "the constant $\log 2$ scaling", but the equation a reader would implement does not contain it, so the correction is short by $\log 2 \cdot \dim$). Suggested fix: write $\log\pi(a|s) = \log\mathcal N(u;\mu,\sigma) - \sum_i \log\big(2(1-\tanh^2 u_i)+\epsilon\big)$.

6. `docs/chapter07_actor_critic/actor-critic.md` (7.2) — "In the previous two sections we met the [advantage function](./advantage-function) $A(s,a)$ and the [training method for the Critic](./critic-training)." and `docs/chapter07_actor_critic/critic-training.md` (7.3) — "In the previous section, we defined the advantage function $A(s,a) \approx \delta …$" — both sentences are false under the numbering the writer themselves set (sidebar: 7.1 Advantage Function, 7.2 Actor-Critic Updates, 7.3 Critic Training Details): in 7.2 the Critic-training page (7.3) comes *after*, and in 7.3 the previous section is 7.2 Actor-Critic, not 7.1 Advantage. 7. `docs/chapter07_actor_critic/actor-critic.md` — "The Critic update $V(s) \leftarrow V(s) + \alpha\delta$ is the [TD-learning](../chapter04_classical_methods/dp-mc-td) update from Chapter 3 with a neural function approximator." — the link points at Chapter 4 ("4.1 Dynamic Programming, Monte Carlo and TD"), and the TD(0) update $V(s)\leftarrow V(s)+\alpha[r+\gamma V(s')-V(s)]$ is introduced there; Chapter 3 ("Value Functions & Bellman Equations") only covers Bellman equations, so "from Chapter 3" contradicts its own hyperlink. ## Doubts

1. `docs/chapter07_actor_critic/ac-frontier.md` — "the parameter count grows from thousands to well over a hundred million" (AlphaStar). The Nature paper (Vinyals et al., 2019) does not publish a parameter count, so even the softened sentence is more precise than the record supports; it also sits next to the "Critic … conceptually the same Critic we studied in this chapter" phrasing that invites the reader to believe the comparison is exact. Safer: "from a few thousand parameters to a network two orders of magnitude larger".

2. `docs/chapter07_actor_critic/ac-frontier.md` — the SAC robot table rows and the Figure 4 caption. The writer softened the lead-in ("SAC-style maximum-entropy methods have been applied to a range of … tasks") but left the rows ("Sawyer … from random policy to 90%+ grasp success rate", "turning a door handle | Franka Emika", "ANYmal … learns multiple gaits") and the caption "SAC deployed directly on physical robots for dexterous manipulation". The 2018 SAC paper / BAIR blog report Shadow Hand pen rotation and a Sawyer door-opening/grasping task; "90%+ grasp success rate" and the ANYmal row are not attributable to SAC from any source I can check offline. Same for the summary table row "SAC on real robots … deployed directly on physical robot arms".

3. `docs/chapter07_actor_critic/ac-frontier.md` — "the league maintains on the order of hundreds of distinct policies". The AlphaStar paper describes the league (main agent, main exploiters, league exploiters) and population-based training qualitatively; I cannot confirm a league size of "hundreds", and the number reads as an unverifiable precision.

4. `docs/chapter07_actor_critic/ac-frontier.md` — the Isaac Lab table ("2,000-16,000 on a single GPU", "~100,000 steps/sec (single GPU)", "1-8 hours"). Plausible for Isaac Lab but the published numbers vary by task, environment count and hardware; stated more precisely than the cited docs support.

5. `docs/chapter07_actor_critic/pendulum.md` — "$r = -(\theta^2 + 0.1\dot\theta^2 + 0.001a^2)$ … The ideal case — rod upright, near-zero velocity, small torque — yields a per-step reward close to 0." Gymnasium's `Pendulum-v1` adds a +1 alive bonus each step, so the per-step maximum is +1 and a good policy's 200-step return is well above 0 (e.g. ≈ −150 rather than "−500, −300, or even closer to 0"). Not touched by this revision, but it is in the writer's scope and it interacts with the reported −780 ± 39 evaluation.

6. `docs/chapter07_actor_critic/pendulum.md` — front-matter title is "7.4 Hands-on: Pendulum Swing-Up and Balance" while the H1 and the sidebar entry say "7.4 Hands-on: Pendulum Continuous Control". The front-matter title is what VitePress uses as the document title (browser title, search entry), so the two disagree.

7. `docs/chapter07_actor_critic/alphago.md` — the new warning "The listing evaluates the value network even at terminal positions (no legal moves)". In this environment the game ends after two consecutive passes (`is_game_over()` → `passes >= 2`), and a terminal position normally still has legal moves, while `get_legal_moves()` returns `[]` only when the board is full. The MCTS code never calls `is_game_over()` at all, so the warning is directionally right (the value net is always used, the true outcome is never backed up) but the parenthetical misidentifies the terminal condition.

8. `docs/chapter07_actor_critic/actor-critic.md` — "**Critic path**: $\nabla_\theta L_{\text{critic}} = 2\delta \cdot \nabla_\theta V(s) = 2.96 \cdot \nabla_\theta V(s)$. Since $V(s)$ is a differentiable function of $\theta$". The Critic's own parameters are $\phi$; $V(s)$ depends on $\phi$, and on $\theta$ only through the shared trunk of the `ActorCritic` class shown on the same page. Writing $\nabla_\theta$ for the Critic path is confusing even if the shared trunk makes it not strictly false.

9. `docs/chapter07_actor_critic/critic-training.md` (new GAE section) vs `docs/chapter08_ppo/gae-reward-model.md` — Chapter 7 indexes the reward as $r_{t+1}$ ($\delta_{t+k} = r_{t+k+1} + \gamma V(s_{t+k+1}) - V(s_{t+k})$, $G_{t:t+n} = r_{t+1} + \gamma r_{t+2} + \cdots$) while Chapter 8 uses $r_t$ ($\delta_t = r_t + \gamma V(s_{t+1}) - V(s_t)$) for the same object. Both are internally correct; a reader moving between the two pages has to re-index.

10. `code/chapter07_actor_critic/actor_critic_pendulum.py` and `code/chapter07_actor_critic/actor_critic_bipedalwalker.py` — both still print "Chapter 6: A2C Training on …" in their docstrings and console output, although the material is Chapter 7. The writer fixed the page-side figure numbers and the `docs/chapter09_actor_critic/images/` copy path but left the scripts' chapter labels (the report says `code/` was out of scope).

## Verdict

The revision is technically careful and most of its fixes are right: the V-trace formula now matches Espeholt et al. (2018) (the old $\prod \gamma c_k$ really did give $\gamma^{2(t-s)}$), the SAC critic target now has the entropy term inside the discount ($r + \gamma(\min_j Q_j - \alpha\log\pi)$), the "advantage reduces variance" paragraph now correctly notes that a constant shift cannot change the variance of the fixed four-sample slice (both are 32.5, as I recomputed), and every numeric example I re-derived — the corridor DP rounds and the closed form $V(S)=-3.375$, $V(M)=-1.875$, the MC/TD update chains, the $\gamma^t$ return sums in 7.2 ($G_t=4.9010$), the TD/advantage tables in 7.1, the new $n$-step and GAE numbers ($A^{(2)}_0=2.71$, $A^{(3)}_0=3.52=G_0-V(s_0)$, GAE $\approx3.5355$), and all eight exercise solutions except the one qualitative remark in Exercise 8 — check out; the SB3 hyper-parameters quoted in the new text ($\gamma=0.99$, `gae_lambda=0.95`, `vf_coef=0.5`, `ent_coef=0.0`, 8 envs / 16 envs, `n_steps=32`) also match the scripts in `code/chapter07_actor_critic/` exactly. The serious problems are (1) the ac-frontier page now contradicts itself because the softened AlphaStar scale claims were removed in one place and left in the league paragraph and the summary table, (2) the Exercise 8 solution gets the direction of the `.detach()` bug backwards (optimistic, not pessimistic — verified numerically), (3) the new tanh tip in the Pendulum page describes a squashing scheme that neither the page's own code nor the SB3 A2C script uses, and its displayed Jacobian drops the $\log 2$ factor, and (4) the new `review.md` page is not in the sidebar (and uses a non-`X.Y` number the heading tool cannot parse), so it is unreachable. The section-order contradiction between 7.2/7.3 and their "previous section" wording, the wrong "from Chapter 3" TD reference, and the three pages that end with an empty "## References" heading are pre-existing but sit squarely in the writer's scope and were left unfixed.
